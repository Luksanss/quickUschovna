Menu-bar icon

A monochrome template image drawn at 18 pt in a 24 pt menu bar. The motif is a parcel with an up arrow: you’re sending a package, not syncing a folder. It isn’t based on Úschovna’s mark.

What v1 does

Two ways in
Dragging files anywhere opens a 300 × 88 pt drop zone under the icon. It fades in over 120 ms, only for file drags, and closes when the drag ends. The icon itself takes drops too. In Finder, Quick Actions › Send with Úschovna does the same for the selected files and folders. Either way, the upload starts at once.
One drop, one link
However many files a drop holds, it’s one package with one link. Folders are zipped first under their own name. A second drop while one is uploading queues behind it and gets its own link and bubble. Anything over 30 GB is refused straight away.
The bubble
It hangs from the icon. “Link copied” stays about 3 s, stays open while you hover it, and opens the link when clicked. The other versions are the first-run email field, too big, and Úschovna not answering with Try Again. Only one shows at a time.
The panel
Click the icon to open it. It shows uploads in progress with their queue, then Recent links (click to copy, ↗ to open), then the sender email, Launch at Login and Quit. Quitting during an upload asks first. While the panel is open, finished uploads are confirmed in Recent instead of a bubble.
Failures
If the network drops, the upload pauses, the icon and panel show it’s reconnecting, and it resumes where it stopped. If Úschovna fails, the files stay queued with Try Again until you retry or cancel.
For everything
Light and dark mode, the system accent colour, materials and fonts. Animations are 150 ms or less. Every surface hangs from the icon on the display whose menu bar was used. Sizes and dates come from ByteCountFormatter and DateFormatter.
